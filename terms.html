02 What ChargeGuard Does

ChargeGuard provides a real-time fraud detection API designed to prevent card testing attacks on WooCommerce stores. The Service analyzes checkout signals — including IP reputation, device velocity, email patterns, and BIN data — to block fraudulent requests before they reach your payment gateway.

ChargeGuard operates as a plugin installed on your WordPress/WooCommerce installation, communicating with our cloud API using your unique API key.

04 How ChargeGuard Is Designed to Be Used

ChargeGuard is built to do one thing: protect WooCommerce stores from card testing attacks. The way it works — analyzing checkout behavior signals, not storing or processing payment card data — means it's designed for a specific purpose, and using it outside that purpose conflicts with how the Service is built and maintained.

Specifically, the following uses fall outside what ChargeGuard is designed to support:

  • Processing or storing payment card data. ChargeGuard analyzes checkout signals — IP patterns, velocity, BIN behavior — not card numbers. It isn't built or authorized for card data custody, and handling raw card data through the Service would create PCI compliance exposure for your store.
  • Reverse-engineering, scraping, or stress-testing the API. The detection model that protects your store also protects every other store on the network. Its integrity is a shared resource, and actions that probe or replicate its internals put that resource at risk for everyone.
  • Reselling or sublicensing access without written permission. Each API key is scoped to the store and plan it was issued for. If you manage multiple stores or client sites, the Agency plan is designed for that — reselling individual access outside that structure undermines the service quality every user depends on.
  • Using the Service in violation of applicable law or regulation. ChargeGuard is intended for legitimate fraud prevention. Use that conflicts with the laws governing your store or jurisdiction falls outside what we can support.
  • Deliberately sending fraudulent or misleading signals to the detection API. The network's accuracy — including the protection your store receives — depends on clean signal data from every user. Intentionally corrupting that data harms every store on the platform, and we treat this as a serious violation of these Terms.
